## About NEXORA

NEXORA is a unified digital forensics platform designed to simplify the investigation of CCTV and DVR/NVR video evidence.

It brings evidence acquisition, video analysis, metadata extraction, timestamp verification, cross-camera investigation, and forensic reporting into one centralized workspace.

The platform is designed to help investigators organize digital evidence, maintain a reliable chain of custody, and identify important events more efficiently.

### Key Features

- 📁 Case and evidence management
- 📹 CCTV/DVR/NVR evidence organization
- 🔍 Video analysis and investigation tools
- 🕒 Timestamp normalization and verification
- 🔐 Evidence hashing and chain of custody
- 🧩 Cross-camera event correlation
- 🧠 AI-assisted video investigation
- 📊 Forensic reports and evidence summaries
- 🗂️ Evidence board for organizing findings
- 🌐 Modern, vendor-agnostic dashboard
